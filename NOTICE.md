## NIST dataset record

`data/nist_sky130/record.json` is the public metadata record of the NIST dataset *CMOS Sky130 Primitives measured at cryogenic
temperatures* (doi:10.18434/mds2-2997), saved from data.nist.gov. The dataset itself is not included; it could not be downloaded
(see `data/nist_sky130/DOWNLOAD_BLOCKED.txt`).
